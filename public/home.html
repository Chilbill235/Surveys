Questions

Before you start

How do I actually earn money?

Every offer in the catalog is a paid task run by a partner. You complete the task, the partner confirms it happened, and the reward lands on your balance. The catalog page states the exact amount and what is expected before you click anything.

When do I get paid?

The balance updates as soon as the partner confirms the conversion. Most confirmations arrive within a few hours, and some larger offers take up to 24 hours. Until then the offer stays visible in your history with its current status.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

$1.00. Most platforms hold your balance until $10 or $25, which means a first payout can take weeks. Here you can withdraw as soon as you have a dollar on the balance.

How do I get my money out?

Open the withdraw dialog, choose a payment method, and confirm the request by email. Crypto payouts go out automatically once approved. PayPal and other fiat payouts are sent by an operator, usually within a day.

Is it really free?

Joining is free, completing offers is free, and there is no fee on deposits or withdrawals. The platform is funded by the difference between what partners pay for a conversion and what is credited to you.

What if an offer does not credit?

Every click is recorded, so an offer that has been started is never lost. If a partner confirmation is slow or missing, the offer stays pending in your history until it resolves. If it never does, open a support request and the recorded click is the evidence used to chase it.
