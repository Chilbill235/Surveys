Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what RewardZone ("we") stores in your browser and why. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers the personal data itself, and our Terms of Service, which govern your use of the Platform.

1. We set no cookies

RewardZone does not use cookies. Not one. There is no cookie set by this site, and nothing on this page is a workaround for a cookie policy — the browser will show an empty cookie list for rewardzone if you look.

We use two standard browser storage features instead, and they behave differently from cookies in ways that matter. sessionStorage is cleared when you close the tab, which is the privacy win: there is nothing left behind on a shared computer for the next person to find. localStorage survives, and holds only a notification list you could read yourself. Neither is transmitted to us unless you send it, and neither is readable by any other website.

No advertising networks, analytics scripts, social pixels, or embedded third-party trackers run on this site. That is not a promise about the future, it is a description of the present, and it is why this policy is short.

2. Everything we store, in one list

These are all of the items this site writes. Each one is local to your browser; we cannot see them until you sign in and the page sends them to your own account.

Everything the RewardZone Platform stores in your browser
Name Where Why
offerNetworkSessionToken sessionStorage Keeps you signed in. Sent with each request so the server knows whose balance and history to return. Cleared when the tab closes.
rewardZoneEmail sessionStorage The address you last signed in with, so the sign-in form can fill itself in. Not used to identify you.
offerNetworkCreditedDepositsSeen sessionStorage Which deposit receipts you have already opened, so the Platform does not congratulate you about the same deposit repeatedly.
offerNetworkWithdrawalStatesSeen sessionStorage Which withdrawal updates you have already been shown, for the same reason.
offerNetworkNotifications localStorage Your notification list, kept on your device so the bell still has something in it after a refresh. It contains no data about anyone else.

3. Signing out, properly

Sign out in the account menu clears your session and ends the session on this device. Sign out everywhere, in your account settings, ends every session on the account — this one included — and you sign in again next time. If you are on a shared or borrowed device, use the second one and you do not have to think about it again.

4. Clearing it yourself

Every current browser can delete a site's stored data on demand, and most can do it automatically when you close the window. Clearing it means signing in again. That is the whole cost.

5. Do Not Track

There is no agreed standard for what a site should do with a Do Not Track signal, so we do not treat it as a switch. It changes nothing here, because there is no cross-site tracking for it to suppress.

6. Changes and contact

If the list above changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and describe the change in our Terms of Service. Using the Platform after a change means you accept it.

Questions about this policy, or about a specific item you have found in your browser's storage, go through Contact support.
