Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

At RewardZone, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and any information you provide during signup. This is necessary to create and manage your account, send rewards to your balance, and verify ownership of the email address.

Usage data: We automatically collect information about your interaction with the Platform, including which offers you view, how long you spend on the site, your IP address, browser type, and device information. This helps us operate and improve the service.

Data from partners: We receive information about the offers you complete from our Partner providers, including whether an activity was completed and the reward amount. This allows us to credit rewards to your balance.

Communications: When you contact support or we send you transactional messages (such as verification codes or payout confirmations), we retain the content of those communications to resolve your request.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Track and credit your rewards from completed offers.
  • Process withdrawal requests and fraud-prevention checks.
  • Send you service-related notifications (verification codes, payout confirmations).
  • Improve the Platform, including personalizing your experience.
  • Communicate with you about the service when necessary.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for behavioural advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. Cookies and Tracking

The Platform uses a session token stored in your browser's session storage to keep you signed in. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party pixels. Your session data never leaves your browser except when making authenticated API requests, where the token is sent over HTTPS.

4. Data Sharing with Partners

To credit rewards, we share the minimum necessary information with Partner offer providers. This typically includes your Partner account identifiers (not your email address) and confirmation that an offer was completed. Partners are responsible for their own compliance with applicable data protection laws for any data they collect directly from you on their sites.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your account information at any time.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account, subject to retention obligations.
  • Portability: Request an export of your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us through the support form. We will respond within 30 days. Note that deleting your account does not delete the withdrawal history needed for tax or fraud prevention purposes; we retain only what the law requires.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and as necessary to provide the service. We may retain certain information after account deletion for legitimate purposes, including compliance with legal obligations, resolving disputes, fraud prevention, and enforcing our agreements. Specifically, we retain transaction and communication records for seven years for tax reporting purposes.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), hashing with scrypt for password protection, and session management with automatic expiry. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and to log out after each session.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we discover that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

9. International Transfers

The Platform is operated in the United States. If you are located outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. By using the Platform, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Platform after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we will notify you via email to the address associated with your account.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, please contact us through the support form on our website. We will respond within 30 days.
