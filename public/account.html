Password

We cannot show your password, so changing it means proving your address. We email a link that signs you out everywhere and lets you set a new one.

Passkeys

Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device screen lock instead of a password. The key stays on the device, so there is nothing for anyone to steal or for a fake site to ask you for.

    How to set up Face ID or Touch ID

    Passkeys need your device's biometric lock switched on first. If you have never set it up, here is what it takes.

    1. On your iPhone, open Settings and choose Face ID & Passcode.
    2. Tap Set Up Face ID and follow the prompts. You will need the passcode you use to unlock the phone.
    3. If Face ID is already on, skip this. You can check by unlocking the phone and looking for the Face ID symbol.
    4. Open this site in Safari and go to your account settings.
    5. Press Add this device. Your phone shows the Face ID sheet; look at the screen and it is done.

    On an Android phone the same button uses your fingerprint or face unlock, and the wording says "screen lock" instead.

    If the button does nothing at all, check that you are in Safari and not inside another app's browser. Instagram, Facebook and some email apps open pages in a stripped-down viewer where Face ID is not available, and passkeys cannot be created there.
